The Annotation Loop

Every other experiment measures what the pass costs the machine. This measures what it costs the developer — and finds the two in tension. Following the compiler's own repair suggestions to a fixpoint takes one round and five seed lines. It also makes the pass slower.

It converges immediately because SHA-512 is self-contained and libsodium's ref10 is one large amalgamated TU, so neither emits escape records of its own. Before round 1 the entire signing operation ran under DIT inherited from a forwarder, with no placement inside it at all.

Real placement inside SHA-512 and the curve arithmetic means toggles inside the secret lane. Where signing is 88% of the work those are pure added cost and the pass's bill more than doubles. f* moves 66% → 59%. The one band it helps is the middle-low, gaining 4.87 points.

The axis is not monotone. More faithful analysis is more expensive, and the instinct that following the report can only help is wrong.

The burden

Nine of the original 41 told the user to seed a callee they had already seeded — on every rebuild, forever. A seed is a parameter attribute that lives with the function body, so a TU that only declares a seeded callee could not tell “never annotated” from “annotated elsewhere”. Matching is now per argument, so a partially seeded callee is still reported and lists only what is missing.

Fifteen of 41 are indirect and cannot be seeded at all — there is no callee name. That is the floor on what annotation can reach, and LTO does not lift it: measured, LTO does not devirtualize and indirect calls went up 9%.

Seven were SEVERE, all thin forwarders that enable DIT and tail-call, leaking the mode forever. -fno-optimize-sibling-calls — a plain compiler flag, not the callee-saved ABI — takes those to zero at no measurable cost.

The per-page HMAC ran with DIT off and nothing said so. The tell was exact — hmac_init.o and sha512.o were byte-identical between the plain and instrumented builds, meaning the pass had nothing to do in them.

And it could never have been reached by propagation: libtomcrypt calls the hash through hash_descriptor[].process, a function pointer table. It needed its own seed line no matter how good the analysis was. That is the case the information-loss report exists for.
