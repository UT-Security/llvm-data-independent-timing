READING IT
  The ratio is the result; the absolute column is not. It carries the loop's
  own overhead and one counter pair, neither of which is subtracted.
  Overhead near 0 means blanket DIT is FREE on that primitive -- which is a
  property of the workload, and the thing selective placement would have to
  beat to be worth its switches.
