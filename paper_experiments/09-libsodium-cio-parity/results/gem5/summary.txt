How to read it
--------------

blanket is DIT set once before main, no analysis and no switches: it is the
CEILING on what selective placement can save, and on five of six rows it is
under 1.4%. At f -> 100% there is very little to recover.

ExpeDITe, NOP is the same hardened binary with every `msr DIT` replaced by
HINT #0 -- identical placement, identical instruction count, same addresses,
no mode switch executing. It is model-independent by construction, which is why
it has one number instead of two. Where an ExpeDITe row sits close to it, the
switches are not what is being paid for.

Serialising is what real silicon does; renamed is the counterfactual only a
simulator can run. The gap between the two ExpeDITe rows is switch
serialisation and nothing else -- the same binary, one mechanism changed. On the
chacha rows that gap is 35 points, and those are the rows emitting 32 switches
per operation against 2 or 3 everywhere else.

Two caveats, both load-bearing
------------------------------

1. The pass rows are on a denominator that flatters them. -taint-no-tail-calls
   rides on -ftaint-harden, so ExpeDITe pays a TU-wide tail-call disable that
   the unhardened baseline never does: 44 tail calls become bl+ret, +0.89%
   library text, worth -0.91% to +3.49% runtime. Against `rt` (hardened
   codegen, empty seed file, zero switches) the serialising row reads -0.59 /
   +35.71 / +33.39 / +3.29 / +22.22 and the renamed row -1.13 / +1.16 / +3.08 /
   -6.26 / +7.43.

2. Anything smaller than its column's relink spread is not a result. Linking the
   UNHARDENED library K bytes later in .text -- unreachable padding, identical
   instruction stream, identical committed instruction count -- moves these
   benchmarks 5.40 / 4.53 / 7.04 / 0.50 / 1.94 points (no ensemble exists for
   argon2id). Six unreachable NOPs move chacha decrypt +6.18%. That is why the
   NOP row goes NEGATIVE on two columns: a build doing strictly more work cannot
   be 6.33% faster, so that number is an address artifact. Over 12 link offsets
   it becomes -0.15%. Working: docs/results/dit-layout-lottery-2026-09-06.md.

The serialising rows and the blanket comparison clear both caveats by tens of
points. No renamed difference under about five points survives them.
