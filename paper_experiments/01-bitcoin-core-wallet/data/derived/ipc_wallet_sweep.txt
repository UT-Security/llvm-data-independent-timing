IPC change = 1/(1 + time cost) - 1, exact because the instruction stream
is identical between arms. Negative = blanket DIT retires fewer
instructions per cycle. '+/- drift' is the duplicate-baseline arm, the
frequency-drift and noise budget on that figure.

Absolute IPC is NOT measurable on this machine (no PMU access).
